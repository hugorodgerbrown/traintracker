Live GB train times in Claude and ChatGPT

Traintrackr is a connector that answers questions about National Rail trains: which platform, what is running late, and how to get from one station to another.

Traintrackr is free to use. You sign in with an email address; there is no password.

Add it to Claude

  1. Open Settings, then Connectors, and choose Add custom connector.
  2. Give it the name Traintrackr and paste the connector address. Leave the OAuth fields empty.
  3. Choose Connect. Claude opens the Traintrackr sign-in page.
  4. Enter your email address, then the six-digit code sent to it.

Add it to ChatGPT

  1. Open the Plugins page and choose the plus button.
  2. Give it the name Traintrackr and, under Connection, paste the connector address.
  3. Create the connection. ChatGPT opens the Traintrackr sign-in page.
  4. Enter your email address, then the six-digit code sent to it.

Things to ask

Which platform is the next train from Cambridge to Kings Cross?

What are the next trains from Liverpool Street to Colchester, and are they on time?
